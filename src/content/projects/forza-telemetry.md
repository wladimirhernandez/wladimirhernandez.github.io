A Windows app that reads the game's Data Out feed and turns it into a live dashboard: tire temperatures, shift lights, G-force, pedal and steering inputs and engine data.

It times runs and laps with a live delta against your best, records a drive and replays it at 0.5x to 4x, draws your route on a map and suggests tuning changes from your own numbers. It ships as a portable zip and an installer, in English and Spanish.
